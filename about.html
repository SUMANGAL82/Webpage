Biography

I am currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the National Institute of Technology Manipur, India. Prior to joining NIT Manipur, I worked as a Research Associate at the Indian Statistical Institute, Delhi Centre, and as an Assistant Professor at Shiv Nadar University Chennai.

I completed my Ph.D. in Statistics from the Indian Institute of Technology Tirupati, where my doctoral research focused on copula-based multivariate response models for correlated data with inliers, including multiple-inflated count responses and early-failure observations.

My research interests lie broadly in Statistical Inference, Dependence Modeling, Survival Analysis, Reliability Theory, and Computational Statistics. My work involves developing statistical methodologies for complex and dependent data, with particular emphasis on copula-based multivariate models, Bayesian and frequentist inference, nonparametric methods, and lifetime data analysis.

My current research explores nonparametric inference using U-statistics, lower partial moments, and reliability measures, including the development of a median-based aging intensity function. I am also interested in censored survival data, cure-rate models, latent variable modeling, causal inference, statistical learning, and time-series analysis.

On the computational side, I work primarily with R, RStan, and Python, employing maximum likelihood estimation, Bayesian computation, Hamiltonian Monte Carlo, and other simulation-based techniques for statistical modeling and inference. I am particularly interested in connecting theoretical statistical developments with computational methods and real-world applications.

I welcome opportunities for academic discussions, interdisciplinary research, and collaborations in Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, and related fields. Please feel free to connect if you would like to exchange research ideas or explore potential collaborations.
