Conferences & Workshops

Learning, Connecting, and Exploring New Ideas.

Conferences, workshops, and academic schools have been an important part of my learning journey. They have given me opportunities to explore new developments in mathematics and statistics, listen to different perspectives, share ideas, and interact with researchers and fellow learners.

From academic institutions across India to international gatherings in Nepal, France, and Greece, each experience has offered something valuable—whether a new mathematical idea, an interesting research discussion, a fresh perspective, or a lasting connection.

This page brings together some of these experiences and the opportunities to learn beyond my everyday academic work.

Beyond the sessions

The conversations around a talk often matter as much as the talk itself.

What I value most about these gatherings is not only the lectures and presentations, but also the conversations that happen around them. A question after a talk, an informal discussion over tea, or an exchange of ideas with someone working on a different problem can sometimes offer an entirely new way of thinking.

I look forward to continuing to learn from such experiences, meeting people with different perspectives, and being part of the wider mathematical and statistical research community.
